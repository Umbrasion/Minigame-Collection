KISS, MARRY, KILL: CHARACTER GUIDELINES
Please note that some of these guidelines require lore or story knowledge of the character or their world, and I will not always be an expert on every character that I add. If you see any characters that you feel do not follow the guidelines that are listed here, please let me know.
  • Characters must be of an adult age, or of adult-level maturity.
    • If a character is of a species that ages more quickly/slowly than humans, then that is taken into account.
    • If a character is an adult but exhibits overly childlike qualities, they also will not be added.
    • Small/petite characters are not inherently disallowed; however, looking too childlike will likely disqualify them.
  • Characters must not be real people.
    • This includes self-inserts that are too direct to the original person, or avatars/sonas like VTuber avatars.
    • Characters portrayed by live-action actors will not be included, however the character can be added if they have a non-live-action depiction.
    • Characters depicted in 3D that are modeled after a real person (but not representing said person) are allowed.
  • Characters must have an adequate level of sentience.
    • This includes animals or creatures without human-level intelligence, or ones that act too much like normal pets or wild animals.
  • Characters must not appear majorly/exclusively from adult/NSFW content.
    • Appearances in NSFW spin-offs or crossovers in NSFW content unrelated to their home series are not disqualifiers.
  • Characters must be human-created.
    • AI-generated characters will not be included, whether it be an AI-generated image, or a character that was conceptualized by AI.
